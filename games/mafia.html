How To Play Mafia

1. Secret Roles: Pass phone to learn secret roles (Mafia, Doctor, Detective, Civilian, etc.).

2. Night Phase: Phone is passed to active night roles. Mafia picks a target to eliminate. Doctor picks a target to save. Detective inspects suspects.

3. Morning Sunrise: The town wakes up and sees who survived or died.

4. Town Discussion & Vote: Discuss clues, accuse suspects, and vote to execute a suspected Mafia member!
