How To Play

1. Secret Word: Everyone gets the exact same word except the Fake Artist(s), who get no word!

2. One Continuous Line: On your turn, add 1 stroke to the canvas to prove you know the word.

3. Fake Artist Goal: Bluff and draw strokes that blend in without knowing what the word is!

4. 🔥 Chaos Mode: Enables randomized events each round! Modifiers (5s Timer, Wild Brush Width) have a 30% chance to happen, with a 10% chance for EVERYTHING to turn on at once! Imposter count can randomly be 0, 1, or multiple!
