How To Play

1. Pass Phone: Pass the phone around so each player can secretly reveal their role on the card.

2. Innocents: Learn the secret word. Give subtle clues to prove you know it without spoiling it for the Imposter!

3. Imposter: You do NOT know the secret word. Blend in, listen closely, and fake your clues!

4. Discussion & Vote: Discuss, ask questions, and vote to eliminate a suspect. Play continues across rounds until Imposters or Innocents win!

Chaos Mode Info

Warning: Mystery Spoilers

Knowing the exact mechanics of Chaos Mode may ruin the mystery and fun of unpredictable game moments!

What Chaos Mode can do when enabled:

  • Secret random events can trigger dynamically (such as Everyone is an Imposter, Everyone has the Secret Word, or standard Imposter game).
  • Imposters STILL NEVER see the real word — they only receive a subtle general hint to keep their cover intact.
  • The exact event type remains completely hidden from all players until the final reveal at the end of the game!
